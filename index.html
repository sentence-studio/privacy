Sentence Studio Privacy Policy

Applies to the Sentence Studio — AI Rephrase Chrome extension and its optional desktop helper apps.
Effective date: 12 November 2026

In short: Sentence Studio sends only the text you choose to rewrite to the AI provider you choose: OpenAI for ChatGPT, Google for Gemini, or Anthropic through Claude Code on your computer. The developer runs no server and receives none of your text, account details or sign-in credentials. There is no analytics, advertising, tracking or rewrite history. Your data is never sold.

1. Who we are

Sentence Studio is an independent project by an independent developer. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI, Google or Anthropic. Contact details are in section 10.

3. AI providers

ChatGPT (OpenAI). When you use ChatGPT, the selected text and the rewrite instructions are sent directly from your browser to OpenAI (api.openai.com) using your own ChatGPT account. Sign-in happens through auth.openai.com. Requests are made with OpenAI's store: false option. OpenAI's handling of this data is governed by OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

Gemini (Google). When you choose Gemini, the selected text and the rewrite instructions are sent directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) using the API key you created in Google AI Studio. Your key is sent only to Google, in a request header. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy. On Google's free (unpaid) tier, Google may use submitted text and responses to improve its products. For users in the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the United Kingdom, and for keys with billing enabled, Google states it does not use them to improve its products. Avoid rewriting confidential text with a free key.

Claude (Anthropic). When you choose Claude, the selected text is passed to the Sentence Studio desktop app on your own computer, which runs the official Claude Code app that you installed and signed into yourself. Claude Code sends the text to Anthropic under your own Claude account. Sentence Studio never sees your Claude credentials. Anthropic's handling of this data is governed by Anthropic's Privacy Policy and your Claude plan's terms.

4. Optional desktop helper apps

The optional Sentence Studio apps for Windows and macOS let you rephrase text in other programs, such as Microsoft Teams. They talk to the extension only through Chrome's private native-messaging connection on your computer, and no network listener is opened. They read the selected text only when you press the shortcut or use their text box. They may briefly use the clipboard to copy or paste your selection, and they try to restore your previous clipboard contents afterwards. Selected text and results are kept in memory and cleared when the window closes. Nothing is logged or saved to disk.

5. What we do not do

  • No developer-operated servers: your text, account details and tokens are never sent to the developer.
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising or third-party tracking code.
  • No rewrite history.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of user data, and no use of it for creditworthiness, lending or advertising.
  • No use or transfer of user data for purposes unrelated to rewriting your text.

6. Donations

The Donate with PayPal button opens PayPal's own website in a new tab. No account data or text is passed to PayPal. Any donation is handled under PayPal's privacy policy.

7. Retention and deletion

Selected text is held only while a rewrite runs. ChatGPT tokens disappear when you sign out, restart Chrome or reload the extension. To delete everything the extension stores, remove it from Chrome. Signing out also asks OpenAI to revoke the session. Removing your Gemini key in the popup deletes it from the extension; you can also delete the key itself in Google AI Studio. You can disconnect the app's ChatGPT access in ChatGPT under Settings → Security. Data already sent to OpenAI or Anthropic is handled under their policies.

8. Security

Credentials are kept in memory in an isolated extension worker and never exposed to web pages. Network requests are limited to the specific OpenAI and Google Gemini addresses the extension needs. The desktop helpers accept connections only from this extension.

9. Children

Sentence Studio is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. Use of ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude is subject to the age requirements of OpenAI, Google and Anthropic.
